Features

Everything within reach

Six sections for preparing and running a game.

Games and realms

Parties, players, monsters and notes. Each world keeps its own material.

Maps

Places with descriptions, links between locations, zoom and free movement around the board.

Combat

Initiative, turn order, health, conditions and monsters returning to the fight.

Bestiary

Ability scores, challenge rating and abilities. Unique creatures live apart from the bestiary.

Players and characters

The people at the table and hero sheets: classes, levels, ancestries and portraits.

Data and backups

Export games to .gmpack, import them back, and keep backups on launch and exit.

Data

Your records stay with you

No cloud and no account: games, maps and bestiary are plain JSON files on disk. For moving between computers there is .gmpack export; for safety, backups.

  • ◆ Local storage, no server
  • ◆ Backups on demand or on launch and exit
  • ◆ Move games between computers

About

A quiet tool for the game master

Game Master needs no internet and stays out of the way at the table. Two themes, English and Russian interface, its own windows and lists — no system dialogs.
